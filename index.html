Binoculars

Point your phone at a view. Binoculars labels the towns, peaks, landmarks and countries you can actually see — allowing for hills and the curve of the earth.

Binoculars needs your location to know what's around you. Turn on Location for this site, then tap Start — or try the demo.

Needs your camera, compass and location. Nothing leaves your phone except map requests to OpenStreetMap and AWS terrain tiles.
